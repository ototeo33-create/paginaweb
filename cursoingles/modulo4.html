💡 4. The Grammar Secret: CAN (Habilidad)

Lo fácil de CAN

¡CAN no cambia NUNCA! Es el mismo para TODOS.

  • I can swim.
  • You can dance.
  • He/She can cook.
  • We/They can run.

Negativo: CAN'T

Para decir que NO puedes hacer algo.

  • I cannot (can't) fly.
  • She can't swim.
  • They can't cook.

❤️ 5. Like + -ING (Gustos)

Para hablar de las cosas que te gustan hacer, usa LIKE + verbo-ING.

Me gusta(n)...

  • I like swimming.
  • She likes dancing.
  • They like playing tennis.

No me gusta(n)...

  • I don't like waking up early.
  • She doesn't like cooking.
  • They don't like running.
💡 Consejo: El verbo que sigue a LIKE ya no cambia (no importa si es He/She). Siempre lleva -ING.

✏️ 6. Ejercicios Prácticos

📝 Completa con CAN o CAN'T:

I _____ swim, but I _____ fly.
She _____ speak three languages.
They _____ cook Italian food.

📝 Completa con LIKE + -ING:

I _____ (watch) movies on weekends.
He _____ (play) video games.
We _____ (listen) to music.
