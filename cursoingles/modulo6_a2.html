📖 2. Read & Discover

"If you eat my chocolate, I will be extremely angry! But if you buy me a coffee, I will forgive you."

💡 3. Grammar: The If-Clause

Parte 1: La Condición (Presente)

Usamos IF + Presente Simple.

  • If it rains...
  • If I study hard...

Parte 2: El Resultado (Futuro)

El resultado mágico usando WILL.

  • ...I will stay home.
  • ...I will pass the exam.

🎯 4. Mission: The FBI Negotiator

🕴️

Paso 1: El Secuestrador

Haz demandas usando IF: "If you don't give me a helicopter, I will..."

🕵️

Paso 2: Negociador FBI

Contraofertas: "If you release the people, we will give you pizza."
