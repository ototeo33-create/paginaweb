When it happened

Relata acciones interrumpidas en el pasado usando Past Continuous.

⏱️ 1. Past Continuous

Estructura: Sujeto + Was/Were + Verbo-ING

AFIRMATIVO

  • I was work-ing.
  • You were runn-ing.
  • He/She was sleep-ing.

NEGATIVO

  • I was not (wasn't) working.
  • They were not (weren't) sleeping.

📖 2. Read & Discover

"Hello, Police? Yes, I saw it. I was walk-ing my dog when the accident happen-ed. The green car was driv-ing very fast while the other driver was listen-ing to music."

🔀 3. When vs While

WHEN (Cuando)

La acción corta/interrumpida va con WHEN.

I was sleeping when the phone rang.

WHILE (Mientras)

La acción larga/en progreso va con WHILE.

I was sleeping while the phone was ringing.

✏️ 4. Ejercicios

I _____ (work) when the accident happened.
She _____ (eat) while he was cooking.

🎯 5. Mission: The Witness

Un testigo describe lo que estaba haciendo cuando ocurrió un accidente.

"I was walking my dog when I heard a loud noise..."
