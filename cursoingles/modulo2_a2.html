📖 2. Real Context (The Witness)

"Hello, Police? Yes, I saw it. I was walking my dog when the accident happened. The green car was driving very fast while the other guy was listening to music."

💡 3. The Grammar Secret: Action vs Interruption

Past Continuous (El Fondo)

La acción larga que estaba pasando tranquilo.

  • I was sleeping.
  • They were eating.

Past Simple (La Interrupción)

La acción sorpresiva que arruinó la paz (usamos WHEN).

  • ...when the phone rang.
  • ...when the lights went out.
