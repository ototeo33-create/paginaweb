📝 2. Verbos comunes en pasado

Acciones comunes

  • Eat → Ate
  • See → Saw
  • Drink → Drank
  • Sleep → Slept

Más verbos

  • Speak → Spoke
  • Meet → Met

📖 3. Read & Discover

"Today I am happy. But yesterday, I was very tired. The weather was bad. We were at the cinema watching a boring movie."
💡 Aha Moment! WAS y WERE son formas del verbo TO BE en pasado. WAS se usa con I/He/She/It y WERE con You/We/They.

💡 4. Was vs Were

WAS (I / He / She / It)

Singular

  • I was tired. (Yo estaba cansado)
  • He was happy.
  • She was at home.
  • It was raining.

WERE (You / We / They)

Plural

  • You were at home. (Estabas en casa)
  • We were happy.
  • They were tired.
💡 Forma negativa:
  • I was not (wasn't) tired.
  • They were not (weren't) happy.

✏️ 5. Ejercicios Prácticos

📝 Completa con WAS o WERE:

I _____ tired yesterday.
They _____ at the beach last weekend.
She _____ happy on her birthday.
We _____ in Paris last summer.

📝 Responde usando ayer (yesterday):

Where _____ you yesterday? (at home)
What _____ he doing last night? (sleeping)

🕵️‍♂️

Estudiante A: El Policía

🥷

Estudiante B: El Sospechoso
