📝 1. Past Simple: Regular vs Irregular

En pasado simple, los verbos se dividen en dos grupos.

✓ Regulares: añades -ED

  • Want → Wanted
  • Rain → Rained
  • Walk → Walked
  • Play → Played
  • Study → Studied

✗ Irregulares: memorízalos

  • Go → Went
  • Eat → Ate
  • See → Saw
  • Take → Took
  • Write → Wrote
  • Speak → Spoke

📖 3. Read & Discover

"Last year, I went to Paris. I wanted a perfect trip. But it rained every single day! I lost my passport and I ate a terrible sandwich. I cried a little."
💡 Aha Moment! En inglés, el pasado simple NO cambia según la persona. "I went", "You went", "He went" - todos usan WENT. El problema es memorizar las formas irregulares.

❓ 4. Negativos y Preguntas

Negativo

Sujeto + did not (didn't) + verbo base

  • I didn't go to the party.
  • She didn't eat breakfast.
  • They didn't see the movie.

Pregunta

Did + sujeto + verbo base?

  • Did you go to the party?
  • Did she eat breakfast?
  • Did they see the movie?

✏️ 5. Ejercicios Prácticos

📝 Convierte al pasado simple:

I go to Paris → I _____ to Paris last year.
She eats pizza → She _____ pizza yesterday.
They play soccer → They _____ soccer yesterday.

📝 Negativo o Pregunta:

She didn't _____ (go) to work. (negativo)
_____ you _____ (see) the movie? (pregunta)
