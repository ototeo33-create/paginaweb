📖 2. Read & Discover

"I have traveled to many places in my life. I have seen the Eiffel tower. But I have never eaten sushi. Have you ever tried it?"

💡 3. Grammar: The 3rd Column

Have + Participio

Usamos "Have/Has" como auxiliar + el verbo en Participio (La famosa 3ra columna).

  • I have eaten frog legs. (Yo he comido piernas de rana)
  • She has never traveled. (Ella nunca ha viajado)

🎯 4. Mission: Never Have I Ever

🍻

Paso 1: El Juego

Por turnos, diles algo que NUNCA has hecho: "I have never ridden a horse".

🙋

Paso 2: Confesiones

Si tu compañero SÍ lo ha hecho, debe responder: "I HAVE!", y pedirle detalles.
