📖 2. Read & Discover

💡 3. Grammar: Hay (There is / There are)

There Is (Singular)

  • There is a park. (Hay UN parque)
  • There is one hospital.

There Are (Plural)

  • There are two banks. (Hay DOS bancos)
  • There are many people. (Hay muchas personas)
