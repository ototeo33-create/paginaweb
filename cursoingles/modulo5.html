📖 3. Read & Discover

"There is a beautiful park in my city. Next to the park, there is a bank. Also, there are three coffee shops near my house. There is a hospital, but it is far from here."

💡 4. Grammar: Hay (There is / There are)

There Is (Singular)

1 cosa / 1 persona

  • There is a park. (Hay UN parque)
  • There is one hospital.
  • There is a bank next to the hotel.

There Are (Plural)

2 o más cosas / personas

  • There are two banks. (Hay DOS bancos)
  • There are many people.
  • There are three coffee shops.

✏️ 6. Ejercicios Prácticos

📝 Completa con THERE IS o THERE ARE:

_____ a beautiful park in my city.
_____ two banks near here.
_____ one hospital in this town.

📝 Completa con la preposición correcta:

The book is _____ the table. (encima)
The cat is _____ the bed. (debajo)
The bank is _____ the hotel. (al lado)
