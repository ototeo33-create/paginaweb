📖 2. Read & Discover

"I can't go to the party. I have too much work. I am too tired. I don't have enough energy for this."

💡 3. Grammar Secret: Posición de Enough

Enough + Sustantivo

  • We have enough time. (Tenemos suficiente tiempo).

Adjetivo + Enough

Ojo: Con adjetivos se pone al revés.

  • I am not good enough. (No soy lo suficientemente bueno).
