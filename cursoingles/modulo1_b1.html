📖 2. Read & Discover

"I am completely exhausted. I have been working on this project for 10 hours. It is raining and it has been raining since yesterday."
💡 Aha Moment! Note that the action started in the past, but the focus is on "how long" it has continued until right now!

💡 3. Grammar Secret: HAVE + BEEN + ING

La Fórmula Maestra

Enfatiza el proceso, no el resultado final.

  • I have been studying for hours.
  • She has been waiting since 8 AM.
