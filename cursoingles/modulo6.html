📖 2. Read & Discover

"Good evening. I would like some bread and I would like an apple juice, please. Oh, and do you have any water?"

💡 3. Grammar: "I Want" vs "I Would Like"

Informal (I want)

  • I want water. (Suena grosero con extraños)

Polite / Educado (I would like)

  • I would like some water, please. (Perfecto para ser atendido)
