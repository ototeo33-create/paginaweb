¡Es el mismo patrón del verbo To Be! Los VIPs (He/She/It) también cambian aquí.

💡 4. The Grammar Secret: Have vs Has

Grupo 1: Los Normales

I / You / We / They → HAVE

  • I have a dog.
  • You have a big house.
  • We have two cars.
  • They have three children.

Grupo 2: Los VIPs

He / She / It → HAS

  • He has a beautiful wife.
  • She has blue eyes.
  • It has four legs.

👆 5. This, That, These, Those

Los demostrativos nos indican cercanía o lejanía.

Cercanía (This/These)

This = Esto/Ese (singular, cerca)

"This is my house."

These = Estos/Esas (plural, cerca)

"These are my books."

Lejanía (That/Those)

That = Eso/Aquel (singular, lejos)

"That is John's car."

Those = Esos/Aquellos (plural, lejos)

"Those are my parents."

💡 Consejo: Si puedes tocarlo o está cerca de ti → this/these. Si está lejos → that/those.
