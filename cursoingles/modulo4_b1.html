💡 3. Grammar Secret: "Solía..."

Used To (Afirmativo)

  • I used to smoke, but I stopped. (Yo solía fumar...)

Didn't Use To (Negativo)

Ojo: se le quita la 'D' al final porque ya usamos "didn't".

  • I didn't use to like vegetables. (A mi no solían gustarme...)
