Bigger, Better, Faster

Comparativos y Superlativos para comparar cosas y personas.

📊 1. Comparativos

Cortos (1-2 sílabas): + ER + THAN

  • Big → Bigger than
  • Fast → Faster than
  • Old → Older than
  • Small → Smaller than

Largos (3+ sílabas): MORE + ADJ + THAN

  • Beautiful → More beautiful than
  • Expensive → More expensive than
  • Interesting → More interesting than

🏆 2. Superlativos

Cortos: THE + ADJ + EST

  • Big → The biggest
  • Fast → The fastest
  • Old → The oldest

Largos: THE MOST + ADJ

  • Beautiful → The most beautiful
  • Expensive → The most expensive
⚠️ Irregulares: Good → Better / Bad → Worse

✏️ 3. Ejercicios

My house is _____ than yours. (big)
She is the _____ student in class. (smart)
This movie is _____ than that one. (interesting)
