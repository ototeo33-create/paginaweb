📖 2. Read & Discover

ORIGINAL: "I am rich and I have a Ferrari." (John)

GOSSIP: John told me that he was rich and he had a Ferrari. What a liar!

💡 3. Grammar Secret: Un paso al pasado

La Regla del Retroceso

Al reportar, todo da un paso atrás en el tiempo. Presente ➡️ Pasado.

  • "I like apples" ➡️ She said that she liked apples.
  • "I am busy" ➡️ He told me that he was busy.
