📖 3. Read & Discover

"Usually, I work in my office. But right now, I am sitting at home and it is raining outside. My dog is sleeping on the couch. The children are playing video games."
💡 Aha Moment! El Presente Continuo describe acciones que están PASANDO AHORA MISMO. Usamos el verbo TO BE + el verbo con -ING.

💡 4. Present Continuous (-ING)

Estructura: Sujeto + To Be + Verbo-ING

AFIRMATIVO

  • I am work-ing → Yo estoy trabajando.
  • She is eat-ing → Ella está comiendo.
  • They are run-ning → Ellos están corriendo.

NEGATIVO

  • I am not working.
  • She is not (isn't) eating.
  • They are not (aren't) running.
⚠️ Error común: NO uses Presente Continuo para hábitos (cosas que haces siempre). Solo para acciones que pasan AHORA.
  • ❌ "I am liking coffee." (Incorrecto - gusto no cambia)
  • ✅ "I like coffee." (Correcto - es un hábito)
  • ✅ "I am drinking coffee now." (Correcto - acción ahora)

🔀 5. Simple vs Continuous

¿Cuándo usar cada uno?

Simple (Hábitos/Rutinas)

  • I work every day.
  • She drinks coffee in the morning.
  • They live in Paris.

Continuous (Ahora mismo)

  • I am working right now.
  • She is drinking coffee now.
  • They are living in a hotel this week.

✏️ 6. Ejercicios Prácticos

📝 Convierte a Presente Continuo (ahora mismo):

I work → I _____ _____ (work) now.
She eats → She _____ _____ (eat) lunch.
They run → They _____ _____ (run) in the park.

📝 Simple o Continuous?

I _____ English every day. (study) - hábito
Look! The baby _____ (sleep). - ahora
