📖 4. Read & Discover

Lee la rutina de Elon. ¿Notas algo diferente en los verbos?

"Elon wakes up at 7:00 AM. He drinks black coffee quickly. Then, he goes to work. In the afternoon, he eats a sandwich at his desk. At night, he watches TV and reads a book before he goes to sleep."
💡 Aha Moment! Cuando hablamos de nosotros mismos usamos "I wake up", pero cuando hablamos de un TERCERO (él o ella), el verbo gana superpoderes y se le agrega una letra "S" al final.

💡 5. The Grammar Secret: Present Simple

Grupo 1: Los Normales 🧍‍♂️

I / You / We / They - Sin cambios

  • I wake up at 7:00.
  • You drink coffee.
  • We watch TV.
  • They study English.

Grupo 2: Los VIPs 👑

He / She / It - ¡Añade la "S"!

  • She wakes up at 7:00.
  • He drinks coffee.
  • My dog (it) watches TV.
  • She studies English.
💡 Reglas para la "S" en VIPs:
  • Verbos normales → -s (wakes, drinks, reads)
  • Verbos que terminan en -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o → -es (watches, teaches, goes)
  • Verbos que terminan en -consonante + y → -ies (studies, flies)

✏️ 6. Ejercicios Prácticos

📝 Convierte al Tercero (añade la S/ES):

I wake up → He _____ up at 6 AM.
I drink coffee → She _____ coffee every morning.
I watch TV → He _____ TV at night.
I go to work → She _____ to work at 8 AM.
I study English → They _____ English every day.

📝 Completa la rutina con el verbo correcto:

I _____ (wake up) at 6:00 AM every day.
She always _____ (take a shower) in the morning.
We _____ (have breakfast) at 7:30.
